## Method (documented, Rule 26)

No paid volume tool was used. This is built from: (a) the legacy FOREXAA
site's own service language, (b) competitor SERP/positioning review
(B2Broker, Leverate, UpTrader, Brokeree, Soft-FX, Devexperts, Finalto,
Match-Trader, FXCRM, FXBO — fetched where reachable), (c) the category
terminology actually used across those sites, and (d) semantic topic
analysis. Search-volume numbers are **not asserted**; priorities reflect
commercial value + current site capability.

## SERP competitor notes (benchmarks, not copy sources)

- **B2Broker**: award-dense, problem→solution narrative, public pricing
  (B2STORE), trademark disclaimer footer, hub + deep-link IA.
- **Leverate**: business-model segmentation (CFD vs Prop), public tiered
  pricing, "About Liquidity" content hub, multilingual.
- **MetaQuotes / cTrader**: platform-vendor positioning, downloads +
  "for brokers" sections.

**Content gap FOREXAA can win**: an honest, clearly-scoped "what's in the
stack" story (CRM ↔ platform ↔ liquidity ↔ payments ↔ risk) without
unverifiable stats — most competitors lean on awards/numbers; a clean,
trust-disciplined site is the differentiator.
