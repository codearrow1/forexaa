# AI-CONTENT POLICY

AI assistance is allowed for: research, outlines, information
architecture, editing, summarization, and structured content drafting.

It is **not** allowed to: generate hundreds of keyword-variation pages to
occupy the index, or publish low-value automation at scale.

## Why

Google's spam policies explicitly warn that large volumes of low-value
pages generated by automation (including AI) — "scaled content abuse" —
can lead to manual actions or removal (Rules 2/3/32). We do not want the
FOREXAA site to be that example.

## Rules

1. **Value first.** Every AI-assisted draft must pass the content-quality
   gate (docs/seo/CONTENT-QUALITY-STANDARD.md) and add genuine value over
   what a human would find elsewhere.
2. **No programmatic mass pages.** There is no "generate the 200
   platform×feature×market combos" pipeline. Programmatic SEO, if ever
   built, is strictly controlled (Rule 66): each generated page needs a
   unique purpose, real content, internal navigation, differentiation,
   editorial review, evidence, and a proper canonical.
3. **Editorial review is mandatory.** An AI-drafted page is not
   publishable until a human has verified facts, claims and scope.
4. **Claims are registry-gated.** AI may not introduce statistics, awards,
   partner relationships, licenses or performance numbers. Those come only
   from the claims registry (verified).
5. **Disclosure of method, not of authorship.** We do not hide that
   assistance was used, but we do not publish "written by AI" as a
   differentiator. The standard is usefulness, not provenance.
6. **No spun/synonym duplicates.** One URL per intent; no
   synonym-generated near-duplicates.

## Enforcement

- The claims test (`tests/seo/claims.test.ts`) blocks unverified numbers
  from shipping.
- The `seo:check` validator blocks dead/thin/internal-link problems.
- Each published page records `author` + `lastReviewedAt` (the human who
  reviewed it).
